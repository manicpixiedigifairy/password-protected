Entry VIII: Outfit Repeater

Outfit Repeater, Proudly

The 2000s taught us that "outfit repeater" was an insult, and the Lizzie McGuire era made sure of it. This campaign flips it into a flex.

Creators style one hero piece more than one way, the way people actually wear what they buy.

Creator brief. One hero piece, three looks and one journal entry in their own words. The journal page becomes the caption, so every post sounds like a person, not an ad.
